Who we are

An industrial producer and exporter of premium charcoal and pyrolysis by-products.

Industrial production

We produce premium charcoal and by-products such as pyroligneous acid and charcoal briquettes for metallurgical, industrial and agricultural uses and premium BBQ markets.

Responsible sourcing

We work only with legally obtained forest residue and biomass from areas designated for land-use change. We avoid open burning of biomass and create value from residue.

Based in

Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia. Developed with Grupo Zwald S.R.L., which provides operating know-how, technology and commercial channels.

The problems

Good hardwood is burned in the field while industry and farms pay more for less.

Forest residue is wasted

Millions of tonnes of forest residue are burned, abandoned or underused, causing pollution and economic loss.

Licensed clearing is expensive

Landowners with an approved clearing plan (PDM) spend much of their capital on removal, capital their farms then lack.

Industrial charcoal is scarce

In Brazil, growing demand from pulp companies has created a shortage of industrial charcoal for steelworks.

Farm inputs cost too much

High fertiliser and agrochemical prices cut farm margins and raise the need for local, sustainable alternatives.

Our solution

Turn forest residue into industrial products

Controlled pyrolysis converts forest residue into high-value industrial products.

Constant industrial supply

Steady, scalable charcoal production for steelworks, especially in Brazil and Asia.

Natural agricultural inputs

A biomass-derived bio-input that reduces dependence on agrochemicals and supports more sustainable farming.

02 · Agricultural and bio-input line

Pyroligneous acid

Pyroligneous acid, also known as wood vinegar, is a liquid by-product obtained during the pyrolysis of biomass. Recovering it lets us use the entire production process, producing an input with agricultural, forestry and industrial applications and adding value to the operation.

Key characteristics

  • Activates and stimulates soil microbiology
  • Helps balance soil pH
  • Improves nutrient uptake by plants
  • Can be used as an agricultural biostimulant
  • Acts as a natural insect repellent
  • Can support the control of soil fungi and diseases
  • Applied diluted with water, liquid compost, biofertilisers or organic preparations
  • Helps reduce dependence on synthetic chemicals
Application · Agriculture

Crop protection, natural pest control and fewer chemical inputs. Output per plant: 12.97 t / day (projected).

Process

Carbonisation is a temperature curve. We run it inside steel.

A retort heats wood without air. Each stage happens at its own temperature, and every stage is read off a gauge on the vessel.

Operating model

From biomass to final product, in six steps.

Every project starts with a legal document, not a chainsaw. We only work on land with an approved change-of-use plan, under a contract with the owner.

  1. 1

    Forest study and legal confirmation

    Title check, satellite forest study and a field census with foresters.

  2. 2

    Organised clearing and biomass sorting

    Mechanised clearing of the approved area; biomass is classified before processing.

  3. 3

    Wood processing

    Logs are cut and prepared on site for loading into the retorts.

  4. 4

    Industrial carbonisation

    Controlled pyrolysis in metal retorts.

  5. 5

    By-product separation and processing

    Pyroligneous acid is condensed; charcoal fines go to briquetting.

  6. 6

    Packaging, logistics and export

    Bagged on site and shipped to industrial and international buyers.
